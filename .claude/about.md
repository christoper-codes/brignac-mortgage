### ABOUT THE BUSINESS

Brignac Mortgage is a mortgage lending and financing company based in Louisiana, USA. The company helps individuals, families, real estate investors, and businesses find and secure financing solutions for residential and commercial real estate.

Brignac Mortgage offers a wide range of mortgage and financing products, including home purchase loans, refinancing, construction loans, manufactured/mobile home financing, investment property loans, HELOCs, and commercial real estate financing.

The company works with a network of wholesale lenders, investors, and financing partners, allowing its team to match clients with loan programs that fit their specific financial situation and goals.

### PRIMARY AUDIENCES

1. Homebuyers looking to purchase their first or next home.
2. Homeowners interested in refinancing or accessing home equity.
3. Real estate investors looking for financing for investment properties.
4. People purchasing manufactured or mobile homes.
5. Clients building a new home and needing construction financing.
6. Businesses and investors looking for commercial real estate financing.
